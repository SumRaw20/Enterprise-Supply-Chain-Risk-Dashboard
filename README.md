# Enterprise Supply Chain Risk & Maritime Telemetry Command Center (Node T8)

> **Live Interactive Dashboard:** [View Live Deployment](https://SumRaw20.github.io/Enterprise-Supply-Chain-Risk-Dashboard)

---

## Executive Overview
A real-time, browser-native supply chain risk command center engineered to evaluate geopolitical, regulatory, and maritime logistics disruptions across cross-border pharmaceutical trade flows (China to India). 

The platform models the financial and operational impact of statutory pricing revisions under the Drugs (Price Control) Order (DPCO 2013) and monitors physical maritime transit chokepoints to defend margins and secure critical-care inventory buffers.

---

## Key Operational Modules & Capabilities

### 1. Upstream Maritime Telemetry & Chokepoint Tracking
* **11 Active Consignment Corridors:** Live-tracked AIS routing from major Chinese export hubs (Ningbo, Qingdao, Tianjin, Shenzhen) to critical Indian gateways (Nhava Sheva/JNPT, Mundra, Visakhapatnam, Chennai).
* **Dynamic Lead-Time Variance:** Models door-to-door transit timelines (20–32 days) and detects route deviations—such as carrier rerouting via the Sunda Strait due to Malacca draft constraints (+4 to 6 steaming days).
* **Port Dwell-Time Telemetry:** Tracks container dwell benchmarks at Port of Nhava Sheva (JNPT) held at 4.8 days against a statutory 6.0-day SLA limit to avoid demurrage and cash lockup.

### 2. Regulatory Governance & Statutory Price Tracking
* **Statutory DPCO/WPI Indexation:** Tracks annual Wholesale Price Index (WPI Chapter 30) trajectory (+1.84%) against the NPPA 1.50% statutory threshold.
* **Automated Price-Master Lock:** Incorporates a dual-authorization statutory master-lock protocol (CFO + CCO sign-off) under the Essential Commodities Act (ECA) to prevent administrative margin lag.
* **Automated Stakeholder Dispatch:** Features an integrated client advisory generator to notify 142 institutional hospital procurement networks 48 hours prior to official NPPA gazette adjustments.

### 3. Multi-Echelon Capacity & Risk Scenarios
* **Depot Utilization Telemetry:** Cold-chain monitoring across Bhiwandi Central (82% capacity), Hyderabad Depot (65%), and Chennai Cold Store (40%).
* **Scenario Modeling Engine:** Evaluates 3-tier probability matrices (Best, Base, Worst cases) across Admiralty-graded intelligence indicators (A1 to F6) to project capital exposure.
* **Strategic AMTA Framework:** Categorizes vulnerabilities across the 7-Pillars of impact (Intellectual, Financial, Logistics, Reputation, Regulatory, Physical Infrastructure, and Legal compliance).
